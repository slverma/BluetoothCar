BluetoothCar is a simple and reliable app that lets you control a Bluetooth-enabled car directly from your Android smartphone.

Using an easy-to-use directional control interface, you can drive your car forward, backward, left, and right in real time. The app connects securely to nearby Bluetooth devices and sends control commands instantly, making it ideal for DIY projects, robotic cars, and learning purposes.

Key Features

- Easy Bluetooth connection to supported devices

- Directional controls for smooth car movement

- Simple and clean user interface

- Works offline without internet access

- No data collection or tracking

Permissions

Bluetooth permission is required to discover and connect to nearby Bluetooth devices.
Location permission may be requested by Android to scan for Bluetooth devices; however, the app does not collect, store, or track location data.

BluetoothCar is designed for hobbyists, students, and anyone interested in controlling a Bluetooth-based car using their mobile device
