Bluetooth Car Controller is a simple Android remote control app for Bluetooth-enabled toy cars, RC cars, and DIY robotic cars. Connect your Android smartphone to a compatible Bluetooth car and control its movement using the on-screen controls.

Drive your car forward, backward, left, and right in real time. The app sends control commands directly to the connected Bluetooth device, making it useful for Bluetooth-controlled toy cars, DIY electronics projects, robotics, and educational experiments.

🚗 FEATURES

🎮 Control Bluetooth RC cars using your Android smartphone

⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️ Easy directional controls for forward, backward, left, and right movement

🔵 Connect to compatible Bluetooth-enabled cars and devices

⚡ Send control commands in real time

📱 Simple and easy-to-use interface

📡 Works without an internet connection

🔒 No data collection or tracking

👨‍🔧 FOR HOBBYISTS AND MAKERS

BluetoothCar is designed for Bluetooth-controlled toy car owners, DIY RC car enthusiasts, Arduino and ESP32 projects, robotics hobbyists, electronics enthusiasts, students, makers, and developers building Bluetooth-controlled vehicles.

🔐 PERMISSIONS

Bluetooth: Required to discover, connect to, and communicate with compatible Bluetooth devices.

Location: Some Android versions may require location permission for Bluetooth device discovery. BluetoothCar does not collect, store, or track your location.

ℹ️ IMPORTANT

BluetoothCar is a remote control app, not a car simulator. You need a compatible Bluetooth-enabled toy car, RC car, or DIY Bluetooth-controlled vehicle to use the app.

⚠️ Compatibility may vary depending on the Bluetooth module and control protocol used by your car or project.
